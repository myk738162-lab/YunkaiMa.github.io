📖 个人简介

面向“制造强国”战略需求，长期从事机器人三维感知与自主精密作业研究，提出了多场景高精度感知与智能操作方法，解决了微装配机器人智能控制、FAST靶标三维感知与柔顺拆装、大型舱段自主精密对接、复杂空间曲线焊缝自主焊接等关键技术难题，在国家重大项目中得到应用并获得用户认可；作为项目负责人主持国家科技重大专项课题、国家重点研发计划子课题、国家自然科学基金青年基金等；担任中国人工智能学会智能机器人专委会委员、中国指挥与控制学会无人系统专委会委员；在机器人和自动化领域发表高水平学术论文20余篇，其中以第一/通讯作者身份发表顶刊/顶会15篇，涉及机器人、自动化领域的国际刊物IEEE TII、TIE、TASE、TIM等，人工智能、模式识别领域的国际刊物PR、ESWA、MIR等, 国际顶级会议ICCV、ICRA、CVPR、CASE等；目前已申请国家发明专利20余件，授权10余件，已登记软件著作权10余件，参与制定国家标准1项。

📖 Biography

Facing the strategic demand of "manufacturing power", engaged in robot 3D perception and autonomous operation, proposed multi-scene high-precision methods, solved key issues: micro-assembly, FAST target, large cabin docking, complex welding. Principal investigator of National Major S&T Projects, NSFC Youth Fund, etc. Committee member of CAAI & CCCC. He has published more than 20 high-level academic papers in the field of robotics and automation, including 15 top journals/conferences as the first correspondent, involving international journals such as IEEE TII, TIE, TASE and TIM in the field of robotics and automation, international journals such as PR, ESWA and MIR in the field of artificial intelligence and pattern recognition, and international conferences such as ICCV, ICRA, CVPR and CASE. At present, it has applied for more than 20 national invention patents, registered more than 10 software copyrights, and participated in the formulation of one national standard.
